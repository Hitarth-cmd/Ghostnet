# Marine Protected Areas and Debris Risk (DEMO knowledge-base document)

Marine Protected Areas (MPAs) are designated zones where human activity
is regulated to conserve marine ecosystems, habitats, and species. Debris
that drifts into an MPA poses elevated ecological risk because these
zones typically contain sensitive or high-biodiversity habitat - coral
reefs, seagrass beds, nesting beaches, or critical feeding grounds for
protected species.

When assessing debris risk near an MPA, relevant factors include: whether
the debris trajectory intersects the MPA boundary, the debris's
proximity to sensitive sub-habitats within the MPA (such as reef zones),
and the forecast uncertainty of the drift trajectory - a wide uncertainty
cone that still substantially overlaps an MPA is itself a risk signal
even if the single most likely trajectory narrowly misses the boundary.
