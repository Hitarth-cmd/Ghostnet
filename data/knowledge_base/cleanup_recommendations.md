# General Cleanup and Monitoring Recommendations (DEMO knowledge-base document)

General decision-support practice for marine debris response programs
includes: verifying automated detections before dispatching recovery
resources; prioritizing debris that intersects protected areas or
sensitive habitat; accounting for forecast uncertainty rather than
treating a single predicted trajectory as certain; and recording recovery
outcomes to improve future prioritization.

These are general decision-support practices, not a substitute for an
organization's own operational protocols or the judgment of on-the-water
responders.
