# Coral Reefs and Marine Debris (DEMO knowledge-base document)

Derelict fishing gear can physically damage coral reef structures by
smothering, abrading, or breaking coral colonies as it drifts or settles
on the reef. Because reef-building corals grow slowly, physical damage
from entangled gear can take years to decades to recover from, and
repeated debris incidents can compound reef stress alongside bleaching
and other pressures.

Cleanup prioritization near reef zones typically favors rapid response,
since gear that settles onto living coral is more likely to cause lasting
structural damage the longer it remains in place.
