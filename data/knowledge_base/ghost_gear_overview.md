# Ghost Fishing Gear (DEMO knowledge-base document)

Ghost fishing gear refers to fishing nets, lines, traps, and other gear
that has been lost, abandoned, or discarded at sea. Because synthetic
fishing gear is highly durable, it can continue to entangle and kill
marine animals - including turtles, dolphins, and fish - for years after
it is lost. Ghost gear is a major contributor to marine plastic pollution
by mass and is considered one of the most harmful forms of marine debris
to wildlife because of its capacity for continued "ghost fishing" long
after being lost.

Ghost gear tends to accumulate in ocean current convergence zones and
can drift for very long distances, making its impact hard to localize to
a single origin. Recovery operations typically prioritize gear that is
entangling wildlife, drifting toward sensitive habitats such as coral
reefs or turtle nesting beaches, or accumulating in marine protected
areas.
